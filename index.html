04 / Process

Simple stages. Intentional decisions. Clean delivery.

A straightforward workflow keeps the creative direction clear and reduces unnecessary revisions.

01

Define the direction

Clarify the audience, objective, references, required formats, and the feeling the work needs to communicate.

02

Build the visual system

Establish pacing, hierarchy, typography, color, framing, and repeatable rules before polishing every detail.

03

Edit and refine

Shape the strongest version, review for clarity and consistency, then refine transitions, spacing, sound, and finishing.

04

Deliver for the platform

Export organized, platform-ready files and keep the system practical for future edits, versions, and campaign rollouts.
